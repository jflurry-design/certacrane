CertaCrane Support

CertaCrane is a crane and hoist inspection app for iPhone and iPad. Fill in checklists, add photos, sign, and produce a PDF report, all on your device. Here are answers to common questions and how to reach us.

Contact us

Questions or problems? Email craneinspectorapp@mail.grokbot.com. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

It helps to include your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version and the app version. You'll find the version at the bottom of Settings in the app, under About. Please don't send customer reports or inspection photos unless we ask for something specific. We never ask for your Apple Account password.

Subscription & purchases

How much is CertaCrane Pro, and what does it include?

CertaCrane Pro has two plans: $499 per year or $99 per month (US prices; the App Store shows the price in your country before you buy). Yearly works out to about $41.58 a month, which is $689 less than paying monthly for 12 months ($1,188).

Both plans include the same things: starting new inspections of every type in the app and generating signed PDF reports with your company name and logo. A subscription is needed to start an inspection and to generate its final report.

Both plans are auto-renewing subscriptions sold through Apple. They renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Full details are in the Terms of Use.

How does the free trial work?

New subscribers get a 7-day free trial with either plan. You pick Monthly or Yearly when you start, and you aren't charged during the 7 days. If you don't cancel, the plan you picked starts automatically when the trial ends and your Apple Account is charged.

To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends (see How do I cancel?). The free trial is available once per Apple Account, for either plan.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes. Go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CertaCrane and choose the other plan. Apple shows you when the change takes effect before you confirm. You only have one plan at a time.

Can my family or my crew share one subscription?

CertaCrane Pro doesn't support Apple Family Sharing, so each person who inspects needs their own subscription on their own Apple Account. If your company gives its inspectors codes, see Offer codes.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your completed reports stay on your device, and you can still open, share and export them. Drafts stay saved too. You'll need an active subscription again to start a new inspection or to generate the final report for a draft.

Restoring purchases

How do I restore my subscription on a new or reinstalled device?

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with. Then, in the app, open Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases. The subscription screen has a Restore Purchases button too. Pro unlocks as soon as Apple confirms your subscription.

Restoring brings back your subscription, not your inspections. Drafts, reports and photos are stored only on the device where you made them. See Moving to a new iPhone or iPad.

I subscribed, but the app still asks me to subscribe.
  • Check that you're signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe (iOS Settings → your name).
  • Connect to Wi-Fi or cellular and tap Restore Purchases.
  • If your purchase needed approval (for example, Ask to Buy), Pro unlocks once it's approved.
  • Check iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions to confirm the subscription is active.

Still stuck? Email us with the date you subscribed. Please don't send payment details.

Offer codes

How do I redeem a code?

In the app, open Settings → Subscription → Redeem Code, or tap Redeem Code on the subscription screen. Apple's redemption sheet opens; type or paste the code and confirm. Pro unlocks right away.

You can also redeem a code in the App Store app: tap your profile picture, then Redeem Gift Card or Code.

My company gave me a code. What happens when it runs out?

A code gives you CertaCrane Pro for the period it covers, such as a free year. Apple shows the code's terms on the redemption sheet before you confirm, including whether it renews afterwards. Company codes don't renew on their own, so you won't be charged when the code period ends. To keep Pro after that, subscribe to a plan or get a new code. You can check when your access ends in Settings → Subscription in the app, or in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

My code won't redeem.
  • Check the code for typos. Characters such as O and 0, or I and 1, are easy to mix up.
  • Each code works once. It may also have expired, or it may be for a different country's App Store.
  • Some codes are only for people who haven't subscribed before, or can't be used while another subscription is active.

If you got the code from your company, ask them for a new one. Otherwise email us.

Cancelling & refunds

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CertaCrane and tap Cancel Subscription. While you're subscribed you can also open Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription in the app.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial ends or your renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the trial or the period you've paid for.

Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't cancel subscriptions or issue refunds ourselves.

Reports & export

How do I send a report to a customer?

From the home screen, open Reports, tap the report, then tap Share / Send. The iOS share sheet opens, so you can email it, send it in Messages, AirDrop it, save it to Files or print it. Reports are PDF files.

How do I export all my reports at once?

Open Settings → Data Management → Export All Completed Reports. The share sheet opens with every completed PDF, so you can save them all to Files, a cloud drive or a computer in one go. Exporting works even without an active subscription.

How are report files named?

You choose. In Settings → Data Management → File Naming Convention, pick up to two setup fields (for example, the customer and the unit). The date is added to the end. A preview shows what the file name will look like.

How do I get my logo on reports?

Open Settings → Company Logo. You can take a photo, pick one from your photo library, or import a PNG, JPEG or HEIC file from Files. Your logo appears in the header of every page of new reports. Reports you've already made don't change.

Why do I have to sign before I can finish?

A finished report is the inspector's record, so the app asks for a signature before it generates the PDF. You can draw your signature on the screen or upload a picture of it. The signature appears on that report.

Your data

Where is my data stored? Do I need an account?

There's no account and no CertaCrane server. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone or iPad: drafts are saved as files in the app, finished reports are PDF files, and photos stay in the app's own storage. We can't see any of it. Reports leave your device only when you share or export them. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Because nothing syncs, an inspection started on your iPhone won't appear on your iPad.

Moving to a new iPhone or iPad

The app's data normally comes across when you set up the new device from an iCloud backup or a computer backup of the old one, or with Quick Start. If you aren't moving that way, export your reports first (Settings → Data Management → Export All Completed Reports) and save them somewhere safe, such as Files or iCloud Drive. Finish or note any drafts before you switch.

Then install CertaCrane on the new device and tap Restore Purchases to bring back your subscription.

How do I delete my data?
  • A draft or a report: in Drafts or Reports, swipe left on it and delete it.
  • Saved setup history (recently used customer and unit details): Settings → Data Management → Clear saved setup history.
  • Your logo: Settings → Company Logo.
  • Your profile: edit or clear the fields in Settings → Profile.
  • Everything: deleting the app removes all of its data from that device.

Deleting can't be undone, so export anything you want to keep first. Reports you've already sent stay wherever you sent them. We don't have a copy of your data, so we can't recover it for you.

Using the app

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only to fill in a city and state for you: the job location on a new inspection, and your company's city and state in your profile. It's optional; you can always type the location instead.

To stop automatic filling, turn off Settings → Field Shortcuts → Auto-fill location from city & state. To turn off location access completely, go to iOS Settings → Apps → CertaCrane → Location (on iOS 17: Settings → CertaCrane → Location) and choose Never.

The camera or my photos don't work in the app.

Check iOS Settings → Apps → CertaCrane (on iOS 17: Settings → CertaCrane) and allow Camera (and Photos, if it's listed). Then go back to the app and try again.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Inspections, photos, signatures and reports all work offline. You need a connection only to subscribe, restore purchases, redeem a code, use location autofill, or send a report.

Safety

Does a CertaCrane report mean my equipment is safe or compliant?

No. CertaCrane is a documentation aid. It helps a qualified inspector record what they found. It doesn't inspect anything itself, and it doesn't replace the judgment of a qualified or competent person, the manufacturer's instructions, or the requirements of OSHA, ASME B30 and other standards that apply to your equipment and job. The inspector who signs the report is responsible for its contents and for any decision about whether equipment is fit to use. See the safety notice in the Terms of Use.
