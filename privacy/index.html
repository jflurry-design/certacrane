Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

The short version: we don't collect your data. CertaCrane has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your inspections, photos, signatures and reports stay on your iPhone or iPad. They leave it only when you share or export a report yourself. We can't see any of it.

This policy explains how the CertaCrane app (“the app”) handles information. The app is made by Jason Flurry, an independent developer in Trussville, Alabama (“we”, “us”). CertaCrane is offered on the App Store in the United States and other countries outside the European Union.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores what you put into it, only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Inspections in progress are saved as draft files (JSON) on your device. They include the crane and unit details, the customer, owner, contact, job number and location you enter, your checklist answers and notes.
  • Finished reports are saved as PDF files on your device. A report includes the inspection details, your photos, your name, email address, company name and location from your profile, your company logo and your signature.
  • Inspection photos you take or choose are saved in the app's Documents folder on your device.
  • Your profile and settings (your name, email, company name and city/state, your logo, color theme, inspection defaults, file-naming choices and, if you leave it on, recently used setup details) are stored in the app's settings and storage on your device.
  • Your signature is drawn on screen or uploaded as an image when you finish an inspection, and is placed on that report.

We don't run servers, and the app doesn't send any of this to us or to anyone else. There's no account and no cloud sync. The app's privacy manifest declares that it collects no data and does no tracking.

Permissions the app asks for

iOS asks for your permission before the app can use these features. You can say no, and you can change your choice any time in iOS Settings → Apps → CertaCrane (on iOS 17: Settings → CertaCrane). The app still works without them; you'll just type or attach things yourself. The messages below are exactly what iOS shows.

Location

The app asks for location only “while using the app”, and only to fill in a city and state: the job location when you start a new inspection, and your company's city and state when you tap Use current location in your profile. It asks for an approximate position (to within about a kilometer), not a precise one.

To turn that position into a city and state, the app uses Apple's built-in geocoding service, which means the approximate position is sent to Apple for the lookup. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy. The app keeps only the resulting city and state text in the field you can see and edit. It doesn't store your coordinates, track where you go, or use location in the background.

You can turn off automatic filling in Settings → Field Shortcuts → Auto-fill location from city & state, or turn off location access in iOS Settings.

Sharing reports

Reports leave your device only when you share or export them through the iOS share sheet: one at a time with Share / Send, or all at once with Export All Completed Reports. Where they go next (Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Files, a printer or another app) is up to you, and those services' own privacy terms apply. The app never sends a report on its own.

Information about other people

Inspections and reports can include information about other people, such as your customers, equipment owners, site contacts and anyone in your photos. That information stays on your device unless you share it. You're responsible for collecting and sharing it in line with your agreements with your customers and the law that applies to you.

Purchases

Subscriptions, free trials and offer codes are sold, processed and managed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, Apple Account details or payment information. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device using Apple's StoreKit. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no third-party SDKs and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, and it doesn't track you across apps or websites. If you've turned on Share With App Developers in iOS settings, Apple may share crash reports and basic usage statistics with us. Apple provides these in a form that doesn't identify you. You can turn this off any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Device backups

Like most apps, CertaCrane's data is normally included when your iPhone or iPad is backed up to iCloud or to a computer. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple. We have no access to them.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply to you, we don't add you to any mailing list, and we'll delete the conversation if you ask. Please don't send customer reports or inspection photos unless they're needed to help you.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from this site. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operations; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete a draft or report: swipe left on it in Drafts or Reports.
  • Clear saved setup history: Settings → Data Management → Clear saved setup history.
  • Remove your logo or change your profile: Settings → Company Logo and Settings → Profile.
  • Delete everything: deleting the app removes all of its data from that device. Copies in your device backups follow your backup settings, and reports you've already sent stay wherever you sent them.
  • Take a copy any time with Settings → Data Management → Export All Completed Reports, with or without a subscription.

Because we don't hold your app data, we can't view, correct, export or delete it for you, and we can't recover it if it's deleted. The controls above put that in your hands. Some US state privacy laws give you rights over personal information a business holds about you. Apart from any support emails you send us, we don't hold any personal information about you. If you've emailed us, you can ask us to delete those emails.

How long we keep information

We don't receive or keep any of your app data. Support emails are kept only as long as we need them to answer your question and follow up, and we delete them sooner if you ask.

Children

CertaCrane is a business tool for professional equipment inspectors. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app itself collects no personal information from anyone. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, for example in a support email, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change would mean collecting any data, we'll say so clearly here and in the app before it takes effect.
